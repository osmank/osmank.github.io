Privacy Policy

Lissaloop · com.osmnks.lissaloop · Last updated 15 September 2026

The short version

Lissaloop has no accounts, no login and no server of mine. Your progress stays on your device. I do not receive, store or sell any personal data about you. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Apple (purchases and Game Center) and to the advertising provider described below, and you can decline ad tracking at the first prompt.

Who is responsible

Lissaloop is developed and published by Osman Kose, an independent developer based in Türkiye. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to osmankose85@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following locally, using the operating system's standard app storage. None of it is transmitted to me:

  • Coins, upgrade levels, your runners' form and loop, prisms, best wave and kills
  • Settings: sound, effects and language
  • The time you last played, used to work out what your runners earned while you were away
  • When you last used each rewarded offer and when the last full-screen ad was shown, so cooldowns survive a restart
  • Which purchases you own, and the loop look you picked

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no cloud save on my side and nothing to restore from me.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, Lissaloop reports your achievements and your best wave to it, and the in-game leaderboard shows the display names of the top players. This uses your Game Center player identifier and display name and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed in, the game works the same; only the leaderboard is unavailable.

Advertising

Lissaloop shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), a mobile advertising mediation service operated by Unity Technologies. Ads are either your choice or come at a natural pause:

  • Rewarded ads, only when you tap an offer: double offline earnings and breach insurance
  • Full-screen ads, only after a core breach or an Ascend, never during play and never in the first minutes of a session

To serve ads, the advertising SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking
  • Device and connection information such as model, operating system version, language and IP address
  • Interaction data: that an ad was shown, viewed or clicked

This processing is carried out by Unity as an independent controller. Their policy applies to it: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

Once, when ads are available, iOS asks whether Lissaloop may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads become non-personalised. The game works exactly the same either way; nothing is locked behind that permission. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

Lissaloop offers two optional, permanent purchases: Lissaloop+ (no full-screen ads; rewarded-ad bonuses come without the video, with the same cooldowns; a gold loop look) and Long Break (offline earnings build up for 12 hours instead of 2.5).

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see and never receive your name, card number, billing address or Apple ID. The app only learns whether a purchase completed, so it can deliver it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What Lissaloop does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no email collection
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location access
  • No selling or sharing of personal data by me

Children

Lissaloop is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided such information through the advertising SDK, contact me and I will help you raise it with the provider.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on my side to export or delete; uninstalling the app erases everything the game stored. For data held by the advertising provider or by Apple, use the contact routes in their policies linked above. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Türkiye, you keep your rights under GDPR and KVKK respectively; write to me and I will point you to the right controller.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it and the updated version is published here.

Cihazında kalanlar

Oyun şunları yalnızca cihaza kaydeder, bana iletmez:

  • Ayarlar: ses, efektler ve dil

Uygulamayı silmek hepsini siler. Bulut yedeği yok, bende geri yüklenecek bir kayıt da yok.

Game Center

Apple Game Center'a giriş yaptıysan Lissaloop başarımlarını ve en iyi dalganı ona bildirir; oyun içindeki sıralama en iyi oyuncuların görünen adlarını gösterir. Bunun için Game Center oyuncu kimliğin ve görünen adın kullanılır; Apple'ın gizlilik politikası geçerlidir. Giriş yapmadıysan oyun aynı çalışır, sadece sıralama görünmez.

Reklamlar

Lissaloop, Unity Technologies tarafından işletilen Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) üzerinden reklam gösterir. Reklamlar ya senin seçimindir ya da doğal bir duraksamada gelir:

  • Ödüllü reklamlar, sadece bir teklife dokunduğunda: offline kazancı ikiye katlama ve breach sigortası
  • Tam ekran reklamlar, sadece çekirdek düştükten ya da Yüksel'den sonra; oyun sırasında ve oturumun ilk dakikalarında asla

Reklam SDK'sı cihaz tanımlayıcılarını (izin verirsen IDFA dâhil), cihaz ve bağlantı bilgilerini (model, işletim sistemi sürümü, dil, IP adresi) ve reklam etkileşim verisini (gösterildi, izlendi, tıklandı) işleyebilir. Bu işleme Unity'nin kendi sorumluluğundadır; Unity gizlilik politikası geçerlidir.

Reklamlar kullanılabilir olduğunda iOS bir kez, diğer şirketlerin uygulama ve sitelerinde izlenmene izin verip vermediğini sorar. Reddedersen IDFA paylaşılmaz ve reklamlar kişiselleştirilmez. Oyun her iki durumda da birebir aynı çalışır; o izne bağlı kilitli hiçbir şey yok. Cevabını Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → İzleme bölümünden istediğin zaman değiştirebilirsin.
